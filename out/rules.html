01 · Preparation

Setup & Deal

  1. Separate the 5 Role cards (SYS-01 to SYS-05). Shuffle them and deal 1 face-down to each player; return unused Roles to the box without looking.
  2. Shuffle the remaining 13 Action cards (Relays, Interference, Overrides) to form the Transmission Stack.
  3. Deal 2 Action cards to each player as their starting hand. Set a 6-tick Dawn track beside the stack starting at Tick 0.

02 · Printing

Duplex Cut Guide

  • Print at 100% (Actual Size) — never use 'Fit to Page' or 'Shrink Oversized Pages'.
  • For double-sided printing, select Duplex: Flip on Long Edge. Backs on pages 2 and 4 are mirrored 3-2-1 to match fronts.
  • Cut along the outer hairline crop marks after printing; each trimmed card measures 2.25 x 3.125 in (57 x 79 mm).

03 · Round Flow

Turn Structure

StepResolution
1. DrawDraw 1 card from the Transmission Stack. If the stack is empty, shuffle the Burn pile into a fresh stack and advance Dawn by 1.
2. TransmitPlay 1 card from hand face-up (or face-down if Spoofed Packet) onto the table bus, paying its Bandwidth cost by discarding cards equal to Cost - 1, or pass to draw 1 extra card.
3. Resolve & TickApply the card's effect, resolve any reaction window, and advance the Dawn counter by 1 tick unless an effect alters the clock.

04 · Victory Conditions

Who Wins at Dawn

SIGNAL VICTORY

Total active Signal Power on the table reaches 8 or higher when Tick 6 concludes.

NOISE VICTORY

Active Interference + Noise bursts equal or exceed Signal Power at Tick 6, OR 3 Relays are locked down simultaneously.

NEUTRAL VICTORY

The Dead-Drop Courier wins alone if both Signal Relays and Noise Bursts finish with exactly 2 active cards each.
