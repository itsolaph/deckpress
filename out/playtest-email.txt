Before You Cut

3-STEP DUPLEX PRINT CHECKLIST

1. Print at 100% (Actual Size) — never use 'Fit to Page' or 'Shrink Oversized Pages'.
2. For double-sided printing, select Duplex: Flip on Long Edge. Backs on pages 2 and 4 are mirrored 3-2-1 to match fronts.
3. Cut along the outer hairline crop marks after printing; each trimmed card measures 2.25 x 3.125 in (57 x 79 mm).
